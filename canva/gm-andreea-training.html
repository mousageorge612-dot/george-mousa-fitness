RULES & PROGRESS
Train smart
1
Weeks 1-2: learn every movement with light weight.
2
From week 3: when you hit the top of the rep range in all sets, add a little weight next time.
3
Write your weights after each session and try to beat them next week.
4
Good form first, heavy weight later.
5
Sharp pain = stop the exercise and message your coach.
6
Tap ▶ VIDEO on any exercise to watch a short how-to.
7
Sleep 7-9 hours. Muscles grow and fat burns while you rest.
8
Every Sunday: send weight average, waist and 3 photos.
